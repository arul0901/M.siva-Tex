# M. SIVA-TEX — Royal Handloom Saree Boutique Redesign

> [!NOTE]
> The website has been transformed to reflect **M. SIVA-TEX's authentic heritage**:
> - **Primary Brand Color & Website Background**: `#4D0007` (Deep Imperial Maroon extracted from the official logo emblem).
> - **Secondary Harmonious Palette**: Radiant 24K Temple Gold (`#D4AF37` / `#ECBA51`) and Logo's Warm Ivory / Beige (`#F8F4ED` / `#FAF7F2`).
> - **100% Saree-Exclusive Focus**: Every category, editorial panel, and product card exclusively showcases pure handloom sarees from [public/assets/siva-tex/](file:///c:/Users/arulb/M.Siva-Tex/public/assets/siva-tex/).
> - **Strict CSS Architecture**: Zero inline styles (`style={{ ... }}`) anywhere in the application; 23 clean stylesheets in [src/styles/](file:///c:/Users/arulb/M.Siva-Tex/src/styles/).
> - **Hero Banner Structure**: Fully preserved in layout with synchronized imperial maroon gradients.
